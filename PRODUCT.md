# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, served by GitHub Pages at https://aaji123.github.io/portfolio/. No framework, no build step, no external libraries or CDN scripts. Confirmed by the owner as the one thing the redesign must keep.

## Users

Anyone who lands on the page, with no single priority: recruiters and employers skimming for internships or a first developer role, game-industry people, and fellow students, developers and friends. The owner chose "all of these equally".

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Aaron, a Computer Science student at the University of Turku interested in software and game development. It presents who he is, his projects, skills, education and how to contact him.

Success: a visitor leaves with a strong, distinct impression of who Aaron is and remembers him. Acting (contacting him, opening projects) can come later; memorability is the primary goal.

## Positioning

The site itself is the demonstration: a student portfolio whose form shows the same curiosity about how software and games are built that the copy describes, rather than a template CV.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All existing content must survive word for word: hero copy, the three About paragraphs, eight projects (title, stack, description, links), skills lists, education entry, contact links, Discord username and footer. Its form, order and presentation may change freely.
- The mugshot (`images/Mugshot.jpg`) must show the face clearly. No effect may obscure it.
- `roblox.html` holds the Prison Mining Game asset gallery (16 pets, two views each) and is linked from that project. The owner did not require it to stay a separate page, but its content and the link must not be lost.
- Open Graph tags point at `images/og.png` on the live domain.
- A seeded generative layer (identity.js) exists from earlier iterations. The owner did not list it as required, so it may be kept, replaced or removed.

## Brand Commitments

- The owner asked for the site to be esoteric, abstract and one of a kind, drawing on artists and musicians, and later to "go crazy" (for example, a front page that looks like a room or a painting). Ambition is a standing preference, not a one-off.
- The owner liked the earlier randomly generated banner; generative behaviour is welcome but not mandatory.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real content: the copy in `index.html`, the mugshot, 32 pet renders in `images/roblox/pets/`.
- Real links: GitHub repos for each project, the Monkey Sage website, the Roblox game link, GitHub, LinkedIn, email and Discord profiles.
- There are no project screenshots, logos, testimonials or employer names. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Be remembered: one distinctive idea that a visitor could describe an hour later beats a polished but forgettable page.
2. The facts stay exact: every sentence and link is preserved verbatim, so reshaping never changes what Aaron claims.
3. The face is the anchor: whatever happens around it, the mugshot stays clearly recognisable.
4. Static and light: everything runs as plain files on GitHub Pages and loads fast on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The owner did not name a standard. The earlier versions held WCAG AA text contrast, reduced-motion support and keyboard access; treat that as the floor.
