# Surface brief: portfolio front page (index.html, with roblox.html as the volume's appendix pages)

Mode: Experience. Audience: anyone (recruiters, game people, peers). Job: leave remembering Aaron; open projects or make contact later. Constraints: plain static files, every original sentence and link verbatim, mugshot face clearly visible.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is volume one of Aaron's own light novel, read as an open paperback: colour insert, title page, contents, prologue, episodes with screentone illustration plates, a status window, an afterword and a colophon. It refuses the dark hero plus project-card grid every student portfolio ships.

OWN-WORLD: A cobalt book-cloth field (#2335e8) holds one open paperback of cool white paper (#fbfaf6) and black ink (#121212). Grey exists only as screentone dots. Hot magenta (#ff2d7a) is the colour insert's printed ground, with cobalt and white focus lines bursting around the photo; it also marks the active thumb tab and hover states, never text. (Amended after the finish review: a cobalt insert vanished into the cobalt field, so the insert is printed magenta to read as its own page.) Dela Gothic One sets display type; Zen Kaku Gothic New sets the text. Running heads, page numbers, a gutter shadow, 【bracketed】 status windows and generated screentone plates.

STORY: The visitor meets Aaron on the colour insert, reads his long-titled title page, uses the contents to jump, reads the prologue, flips through eight illustrated episodes, checks his status window, and reaches an afterword with every contact link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: one open spread across the viewport on cobalt. Left page: the magenta colour insert, the mugshot large and unobscured inside cobalt and white focus lines generated around it, with HELLO, WORLD! as a speech balloon from the photo.
