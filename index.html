About

I'm currently studying Computer Science at the University of Turku. I'm particularly interested in programming, game development and learning how software is built from the ground up.

Most of my experience so far comes from university projects and my own projects. I enjoy working independently, learning new technologies and turning ideas into something playable or usable.

Outside of programming, I've also spent a somewhat unhealthy amount of time playing competitive games. I've reached Grandmaster in Overwatch, Grand Champion in Rocket League, and Master in Apex Legends. So I guess I have some experience with games from the other side of the screen too. I also regularly lift weights at the gym and enjoy reading light novels and comics in my free time.
